About this companion. It helps students and working engineers find the right provision quickly. The guides are written in our own words and summarise the Indian Standards, the National Building Code of India and the Model Building Bye-Laws 2016 (Town and Country Planning Organisation, Government of India). The original form pages and reference tables reproduced here come from the Model Building Bye-Laws 2016, which permits use for educational purposes with due credit.

Building rules are made by your State and your local authority, and codes are revised from time to time. Before you rely on a number for a design, an approval or a legal opinion, check it against the current official text and the bye-laws that apply to your site.
